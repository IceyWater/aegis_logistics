# Daily Parcel-Volume Forecast

## What it does

This model estimates how many parcels may arrive at a selected hub each day in the coming days.

## What information it uses

- Which hub is selected
- Day of the week and month
- Promotions and weather
- Holidays
- Recent parcel counts

## What you see on the screen

On the **Forecast** page, choose a hub, the number of days to forecast, a promotion level, and a weather assumption.

The chart shows recent recorded parcel counts in navy and the model's future estimates in amber. The shaded area shows how far off forecasts were for most of the held-out test examples. The page also shows the busiest forecast day and a suggested courier count, calculated using an assumption of 150 parcels per courier.

## Who might use it

A hub manager or workforce planner can use the forecast as one piece of information when planning capacity. Customers usually do not need to see this internal planning tool.

## Keep in mind

The forecast is based on past records and may miss when conditions change. Later forecast days use earlier estimates, so errors can build up. The shaded area is a guide based on past errors, not a guarantee. The courier count is an estimate, not a staffing instruction.
